01 — About

A corner for curious minds.

I am Chinmaya Patro — an undergraduate student with a major in Geography and a minor in Economics, writing about the questions that sit between the two.

My interests lie squarely in public policy and diplomacy: how states design rules, how those rules meet the ground, and what happens in the gap between intention and implementation. Geography gives me the spatial lens — rivers that ignore borders, cities that concentrate both opportunity and exclusion. Economics supplies the language of trade-offs, incentives and scarce public money.

Policy Corner is where those two trainings are put to work. The essays here are not news commentary. They are attempts to slow a question down: to ask what a treaty actually obliges, why a welfare scheme stalls at the last mile, or how a monument becomes contested ground a generation after it is built.
